Design a GoPro mount that clamps onto a 31 mm round bar (like a bike handlebar). It's a split ring clamp with a 3.2 mm wall and a 2.4 mm split, tightened with an M3 screw and nut. A straight arm reaches about 65 mm out to a standard GoPro 3-prong connector (3 mm fingers, M5 thumbscrew, captive M5 nut on one side). It should print without supports. Put it in clamp.scad and check it before I print.
